# Fact-check: drafts 788, 1286, 2039

Checked against: The Kitchn, dimensions.com, Whirlpool, RTA, Bob Vila, Ideal Home, Heritage, Bosch, ADF vol 1 Table 1.1, MyBuilder, Cornwall FRS. All 10 new internal slugs exist (WP search). No duplicate internal links. Idea counts unchanged. All 788 chart rows, 36/42/48, 60/66, 69–76 and 84 match the sources. Arithmetic is correct (41; 56–60; 66–68; 18 to 14–16; 68; 52).

## HIGH

**788 diagram contradicts the chart and FAQ.** In svg788.py: `keep the top shelf within about 69–76 in of the floor`. The chart and FAQ give the top shelf at about 84 in.
Replace: `keep everyday food below about 69–76 in; the top shelf (~84 in) is for backups.`
Also replace the matching phrase in `desc`, `the top shelf should stay within about 69 to 76 inches of reach`, with `keep everyday food within about 69 to 76 inches of reach`.

**788 z.png is stale.** The PNG reads "42–48 in for two people", but the .py says "more comfortable", and the source does not say "for two people". Re-render it before upload.

## MEDIUM

**788 Edit 8:** `and 60 to 66 inches where a wheelchair needs to turn`. The source says "Accessible aisles… 60 or 66 inches" for "those with mobility aids". It does not mention turning.
Replace: `and 60 or 66 inches for an accessible aisle, for example for a wheelchair user`

**788 Edit 8:** `If your walk-in is under about 5 feet wide`. Two 16 in sides need 68 in, so a 62 in room would fail too.
Replace: `If your walk-in is under about 68 inches (5 ft 8 in) wide`

**788 Edit 10:** `into at least two studs per strip`. Short side-wall cleats often span only one stud, or none.
Replace: `into studs wherever possible (at least two on long back cleats), with heavy-duty wall anchors where a short side cleat misses a stud`

**1286 Edit 4:** `The Bob Vila figures above date from 2023`. The page only says "Updated Aug 30, 2023"; it gives no year for the data.
Replace: `The Bob Vila guide was last updated in 2023`
In Edit 10, replace `according to Bob Vila&#8217;s 2023 guide` with `according to Bob Vila&#8217;s guide (updated 2023)`.

**1286 Edit 5:** `so their doors and the machine doors can open at the same time, as … recommends`. RTA says "at least 36 inches of clearance in front of cabinet doors or appliances for opening full access".
Replace: `Leave at least 36 in of clear floor in front of the cabinets and machines so their doors open fully, as <a…>RTA…</a> recommends. Whirlpool suggests about 4 ft in front of a front-loader for loading.`

**1286 Edit 2:** `that you can reach the lowest shelf without a step stool`. This contradicts idea #14, where a stool is needed above a stacked pair.
Replace: `that, except above a stacked pair, you can reach the lowest shelf without a step stool`

**2039 Edit 3:** `<a…>Approved Document F</a> sets`. It applies in England only.
Replace: `In England, <a…>Approved Document F</a> sets`

**2039 Edit 7 FAQ:** `Ideal Home says a working utility can fit into as little as 0.36 m² if you build upwards with a stacked washer and dryer.` The figure comes from designer Rhian Williams, and she does not mention stacking.
Replace: `A kitchen designer quoted by Ideal Home has fitted working utilities into as little as 0.36 m² by building upwards, about the footprint of one 60 cm machine.`
In Edit 1, replace `Ideal Home notes that` with `a designer quoted by Ideal Home says`, and replace `roughly the footprint of one stacked washer and dryer` with `about the footprint of one 60 cm machine, with a second stacked on top`.

**2039 Edit 5 table labels:** MyBuilder's labels are "Small utility room conversion" and "Utility room extension". "Small extension" is not its wording.
Replace `Converting an existing room` with `Small utility room conversion`, and `Small extension` with `Utility room extension`.
In the FAQ, replace `converting an existing room at about £10,000 to £15,000 and a small extension at` with `a small utility room conversion at about £10,000 to £15,000 and a utility room extension at`.
Optionally add the row `<tr><td>Appliance upgrades</td><td>£1,000 to £3,000</td></tr>`.

**2039 Edit 4 (dryer warning signs):** The signs themselves are supported by Cornwall FRS. Its advice, though, is "clean the filter and check the vent pipe immediately", not "stop using".
Replace the added sentence: `If it smells of burning, leaves clothes hotter than usual or takes longer to dry, clean the filter and check the vent pipe straight away; if the problem continues, stop using it and have it checked by a qualified engineer.`

**Placeholders:** 788 Edits 9 and 11 and 1286 Edit 9 have `alt="ALT"` and `"id":0` / `wp-image-0`. Fill these in before publishing.
