## 6. PAA to add to the FAQ
- **What is the storage space under the stairs called?** In the US it's usually an under-stair closet or stair closet. In the UK it's the cupboard under the stairs or understairs cupboard. When it has pull-outs instead of a door, people call it understair storage or a stair cabinet, and an open version is often called a stair nook.
- **What are some DIY ideas for under stair storage?** The easiest are freestanding cube units stepped from tall to short, rolling crates on casters, and hooks on the tall wall. Confident DIYers can build pull-out drawers on heavy-duty slides; one UK couple spent about £80 after an £800 quote. Never cut the stair stringers.
- **What can I do with the empty space at the bottom of the stairs?** Treat it as a mini drop zone: a slim bench with shoe baskets, a console with a lamp, a tall plant or a small bookcase that follows the first few steps. Keep the full stair width clear at the bottom step so nobody trips on the way down.
- **Can I have under stairs storage if my meters are there?** Yes. Most fitted designs leave a removable panel, hinged door or pull-out in front of the meter and consumer unit, so readings and emergency access stay easy. Your supplier can move meters, but that can be expensive, so design around them first.
- **Do I need building regulations approval for an under stairs cupboard in the UK?** Usually not for simple storage in a two-storey house. If the stair is a protected escape route, as in many three-storey homes, loft conversions and HMOs, the cupboard typically needs 30-minute fire-resisting walls and a fire door. Check with building control first.
