**A. After the first paragraph of "Measure Around Your Washer and Dryer First": H3 "Washer and Dryer Sizes at a Glance" (table)**
- Front-load: 27 W x 39 H x 32–34 D in. Top-load: 27–28 W x 42–44 H x 27–28 D in. Stacked pair: about 78 in tall; plan a space of 29 W x 80 H in.
- Side-by-side set: about 54 in wide, so the room needs at least 56 in. Compact machines: 24 W x 33–36 H x 24–26 D in.
- Leave about 4 ft in front of front-loaders for loading.
- Source: https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/washers-and-dryers/laundry-sizing-guide.html
- Image: none (the table does the job).

**B. After the SVG: H3 "How High to Hang Laundry Room Wall Cabinets"**
- Front-load: machine 39 in + about 1 in gap + counter, so the counter surface is about 41–42 in. Uppers 15–18 in above that put the cabinet bottom at about 57–60 in from the floor.
- Top-load: 42–44 in + 24 in lid clearance, so the cabinet bottom needs to be at least about 66–68 in from the floor.
- Stacked: with an 80 in space and a 96 in ceiling, a cabinet over the stack can be at most about 16 in tall.
- Check that cabinet doors don't hit the machine doors, and that the bottom shelf is reachable without a stool (Lily Ann: https://www.lilyanncabinets.com/cabinet-articles/tiny-laundry-room-try-these-10-creative-cabinet-ideas/).

## 6. New FAQ questions (from PAA)
- **How much does it cost to add cabinets to a laundry room?** Cabinets alone run about $250 to $2,000, with countertops another $300 to $1,400 (Bob Vila). A fuller makeover of an 8 x 10 ft room, with mid-grade cabinets, installation and extras such as a sink or pull-outs, usually costs $2,500 to $6,000, depending on region and finish.
- **Can I put a washer and dryer in a closet?** Yes, if the closet fits the machines plus their clearances. Whirlpool suggests about 29 in wide, 80 in tall and 32–34 in deep for a stacked front-load pair, with 1 in at each side and 6 in behind for hoses and venting. The closet doors must open fully without hitting the machines.
- **How do you organize a very small laundry room?** Go vertical. Stack the machines if the model allows it, add a shallow 12–15 in deep cabinet or shelves above them, and put a slim pull-out in the gap beside them. Keep daily supplies closest to the washer, bulky tools in one tall cabinet and chemicals up high.
- **What are the laundry room trends for 2026?** Laundry rooms are being treated as finished rooms: cabinets that match the kitchen, deeper colors such as navy and green, folding counters, hidden hampers and doors that hide the machines. Mudroom-style hooks, benches and shoe storage are also showing up in combined laundry and entry spaces.
- **What colors are trendy for laundry room cabinets in 2026?** Sage and deeper greens, navy and warm wood tones lead, often as two-tone schemes with light uppers and darker or wood bases. In a small room light cabinets are the safer choice. Dark cabinets work if the walls, counter and floor stay light and the room has good lighting.
- **What is the best layout for a laundry room?** For most rooms a single wall works best: machines side by side under a counter, a tall cabinet at one end and wall cabinets above. Leave about 4 ft in front of front-loaders for loading, and about 36 in in front of cabinet doors so they open freely.
