# Lessons from "15 Days Pinterest Blogging Training" WhatsApp group (export read 2026-10-29)
Source: user's export, 2,614 messages Aug 25 → Oct 29 2026. Instructor teaches mostly via video lectures + voice notes (not readable); notes below come from text messages only.

## What the group does / was taught
- Cadence: instructor: "one pin every ~4 hours" (≈6/day max). Members: ramp 1 pin/day week 1 → 2/day week 2 → ~6/day week 3; the member with best Oct results: "3 pins per day, that's it".
- Spread pins through the day (members schedule every 1–4 h), never all at once.
- Same article: up to 3 pins with DIFFERENT titles/images; several members wait ≥2 days between pins of the same article.
- Link repeat: instructor's plan = repeat old article links only after ~3 months, or on a SECOND account. Members who repeated links early on the same account report the account going down.
- Topics: Pinterest Trends + Pin Inspector + competitor keywords → ChatGPT picks daily mix. ~80% seasonal for the best performer (Halloween/Christmas), covered ~2 months before the event.
- "Layout 10–12" = pin designs styled like trending, high-save pins.
- One board per article/topic is common.

## Drops reported by others (Pinterest-wide pattern)
- Sep 14, ~Oct 8 ("Pinterest update? impressions ~0"), Oct 27–29 (several members: "impressions down a lot"). One Oct account fell completely on Oct 27–28; Pinterest support: no restriction/violation.
- Many accounts drop after ~1.5–2 months. Recovery reports: kept posting normally (no spam) or paused ~1 month → traffic started returning.
- New domains often flagged "link may lead to spam" on first pins; most unblocked automatically after ~1.5–2 months.

## Where WE differ (likely contributors to our Oct 21 drop)
1. Volume: our CSVs put ~10–15 pins/day live Oct 17–31 (vs 3–6).
2. Link repeats: 33 URLs got 2–5 pins inside Oct 15–24, often every 2 days (e.g. under-stairs-shoe-storage 5×, box-room 4×, room-divider 4×) + CSV-48 "fresh pins for winners" = repeating links on the same young account.
3. Up to 7–8 pins per URL overall (gift-wrap 8, studio 7…) vs their 1–3.
4. Bulk CSV upload of 26 pins in one session (Oct 24).

## Rules adopted
- Max 5 pins/day live, spread through the day; target 3/day.
- Max 3 pins per article total for now; ≥7 days between pins of the same URL.
- No "winner repeat" CSVs until the account is 3+ months old.
- Upload CSV files max 2/day; no big batches.
- Seasonal first in Q4: Christmas content pins now (Christmas table pins were our 2nd best).
- No deletes/edits of existing pins; let the schedule taper (falls below 5/day from Nov 3).
