    How to Choose the Right Rug Size

    The most common mistake is a rug that is too small. According to the Home Depot rug size guide, the right size depends on how the furniture is arranged, not just on the size of the room. Measure the furniture group first, then check that the rug still leaves a border of bare floor to the walls.

    1. Measure the room from wall to wall, and note doors, vents and fireplaces.
    2. Measure the furniture group: the sofa, the dining table or the bed.
    3. Choose the look: all legs on the rug, front legs only, or a smaller rug under a coffee table.
    4. Mark it out with painter's tape and live with it for a day before you buy.

    Living Room Rug Size

    The Home Depot guide shows three ways to place a living room rug. Our rug size calculator gives you all three:

    Front legs on the rug is the easiest choice in most rooms: it connects the sofa and chairs without needing a huge rug, and it suits a sofa that stands against a wall. In a small living room, a rug under the coffee table works, but it should still be at least 5' x 8' so it does not look like a doormat.

    Dining Room Rug Size

    A dining rug must be large enough that chairs stay on it when you pull them out. The Fibreworks custom rug guide suggests measuring the table and adding 24 to 36 in (61 to 91 cm) to each side, with 30 in (76 cm) as the sweet spot and 36 in for large upholstered chairs. The rug size calculator uses the chair setting you choose.

    Bedroom Rug Size

    In a bedroom, the rug should show on both sides of the bed and at the foot. The Home Depot guide recommends that it extend at least 24 in (61 cm) from the edges of the bed, and the Fibreworks guide suggests 30 to 36 in (76 to 91 cm) in a larger room, with the rug under at least the lower two-thirds of the bed.

    Most people stop the rug just short of the nightstands so they stand level on the floor. If the room is too small for a large rug, a runner on each side of the bed is a good alternative.

    Hallway Runner Size

    For a hallway, the Fibreworks guide recommends leaving about 4 to 6 in (10 to 15 cm) of floor between the runner and each wall and each end of the hallway. Many runners are about 30 in (76 cm) wide, and US runners usually come in lengths of 8, 10 and 12 ft. For a long hallway, use two runners in a row or order a custom length, and keep the runner clear of doors so they can open.

    Rug Size FAQ

    What size rug do I need for my living room?

    For most living rooms, an 8' x 10' rug (or 200 x 290 cm in the UK) lets the front legs of the sofa and chairs rest on it. In a large room, go up to 9' x 12' or 10' x 13' so every leg fits, and keep about 12 to 18 in (30 to 46 cm) of floor showing to the walls.

    How do you calculate rug size for a room?

    Measure the room and subtract about 18 in (46 cm) from each side to leave a border of floor; that is the largest rug for an all-legs-on look. Then check the furniture: the rug should be a little longer than the sofa, or 24 to 36 in (61 to 91 cm) bigger than the dining table on every side. The rug size calculator above does both checks for you.

    What size rug goes under a queen bed?

    An 8' x 10' rug laid crosswise under the lower two-thirds of a queen bed leaves about 30 in (76 cm) of rug on each side. For a rug under the whole bed, or in a bigger room, choose 9' x 12'.

    Is it better to have a rug too big or too small?

    Slightly too big is usually better. A rug that is too small makes furniture look like it is floating and the room feel smaller. Just keep a border of bare floor around the edges so the rug does not run wall to wall.
