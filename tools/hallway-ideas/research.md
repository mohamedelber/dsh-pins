## Notes / caveats
- #1 is the M4(2) "accessible and adaptable" optional standard, and #2 is the M4(1) and M4(2) tables. Neither is a blanket legal minimum for every existing home. Present them as "Building Regulations guidance for new homes is 900 mm".
- #3 is the Lifetime Homes standard (CAE), not gov.uk.
- #6–9 apply to new builds, extensions and loft conversions (LABC). #4–5 apply to rented homes in England.
- #23 and #27 are retailer prices (Screwfix, Buildworld) and will change.
- The Dulux tips come from dulux.ie (Dulux Ireland). The products are the same in the UK.
- The Little Greene quotes come from littlegreene.us. The product is the same.
- #50 converted from inches: 12–14 in = 30.5–35.6 cm; 36 in = 91.4 cm.
