## Caveats/unconfirmed

- **How quotes were checked.** Every quote passed through a summariser. Most are short sentences the summariser returned as verbatim, but a few may be lightly cut down. Check any quote you will put in quotation marks against the live page before publishing.
- **Named people asked for but not found:**
  - **Joa White Hamilton:** no bedroom quote found. Joa *Studholme* (F&B colour curator) was used instead (rows 14, 23, 27, 64).
  - **Marianne Shillingford:** quoted (rows 51, 95), but I found no specific Dulux colour recommended by her for bedrooms.
  - **Crown:** colours come from Crown's own bedroom page (Irish site, crownpaints.com/ie), not from a named expert.
  - **Mylands:** the post is unattributed brand copy, not Dominic Myland.
  - **Lick:** the eve Sleep blog quote was not attributed to Tash Bradley by name, so it was not used.
- **Little Greene sage north-facing (row 5):** the Valspar expert's view that sage suits north-facing rooms is paraphrased in the source; no verbatim quote was captured.
