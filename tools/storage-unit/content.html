    How the Storage Unit Size Calculator Works

    • It matches your home size to the guides above: a few boxes 25 sq ft, a studio 50, one bedroom 75 to 100, two bedrooms 100, three bedrooms 150, four bedrooms 200 and five or more 300 sq ft.
    • About half moves you one size down, for example when the big furniture stays behind or goes with you.
    • A walk-in aisle moves you one size up. This is our own rule of thumb: an aisle down the middle costs floor space but lets you reach the back without unpacking the front.
    • For US users it shows the nearest standard unit (5 x 5, 5 x 10, 10 x 10, 10 x 15, 10 x 20 or 10 x 30). UK users see the square feet that stores such as Big Yellow sell.

    These guides assume a sensibly packed unit. If you own large furniture, a piano or lots of garden equipment, go one size up. If you are mainly storing boxes, you can often go one size down.

    What Size Storage Unit for a Car?

    Public Storage's guide to storing a car suggests a 20 ft space for a sedan or standard-size car and a 16 ft space for a small car, and describes a 10 x 20 ft unit as roughly the size of a small one-car garage. For an SUV, van or truck, it says to measure first. Before you book, measure the car's length, its width including mirrors and its height, compare them with the unit and its door opening, and check that the facility allows a vehicle in that unit.

    How to Pack a Storage Unit to Fit More

    1. Use boxes of the same size. They stack into stable columns and waste less space. Our moving box calculator tells you how many you need.
    2. Take furniture apart. Remove table legs and take bed frames apart, and stand mattresses on their long side against a wall.
    3. Heavy at the bottom, light on top. Put books and kitchen boxes low and bedding and lampshades high.
    4. Keep what you need near the door. Seasonal items and paperwork go at the front, things you will not need for months at the back.
    5. Write a list. Number every box and keep a list on your phone of what is in each one.

    Storage Unit Size FAQ

    What size storage unit do I need for a 2 bedroom apartment?

    A 10 x 10 ft unit (100 sq ft) holds the contents of a typical one or two bedroom apartment, according to RentCafe. Big Yellow in the UK suggests 100 sq ft for a standard two bedroom flat or house. A large two bedroom home with a lot of furniture may need a 10 x 15.

    What size storage unit do I need for a 3 bedroom house?

    Plan a 10 x 15 ft unit (150 sq ft) for a typical three bedroom house, or a 10 x 20 ft unit (200 sq ft) for a large one. RentCafe says a 10 x 20 can easily hold a three bedroom home, and Big Yellow suggests 125 to 150 sq ft.

    What size storage unit for a 1 bedroom apartment?

    A 5 x 10 ft unit can work for a small one bedroom apartment, as U-Haul suggests, but a 10 x 10 ft unit gives room for a bed, sofa and appliances without packing it to the ceiling. In the UK, Big Yellow suggests about 75 sq ft for a large one bedroom home.

    How big is a 10 x 10 storage unit?

    A 10 x 10 storage unit has 100 sq ft (about 9.3 m²) of floor space, about the size of a regular bedroom. It typically holds the contents of a one or two bedroom apartment, including a bed, sofa, dresser and boxes.
