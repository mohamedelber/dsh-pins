## Caveats/unconfirmed

- **Example sizes "1.8 x 2.1 m" and "2.4 x 1.5 m".** Neither exact pair was found. Confirmed alternatives: 1.8 x 2.0 m (Blueleaf), ~2.1 m long (Habitus, whose width figure has a typo), 3 m² minimum (Houzz AU / Bagnato), 3–5 m² typical (Blueleaf).
- **The 900 x 900 mm shower is convention, not code.** Blueleaf claims "AS 3740 sets the minimums", but AS 3740 is the waterproofing standard and does not set shower sizes. Present 900 x 900 as the common minimum.
- **Livable Housing Design Standard (rows 14–19).** This applies to new homes only in states that adopted it; NSW and WA did not adopt it, as far as I know. Present these figures as accessibility good practice, and check current adoption on abcb.gov.au.
- **NCC floor falls (row 26).** The fetch returned only the ratios 1:80 and 1:50, not a full sentence. Check the wording of clause 10.2.12 before quoting.
- **NCC 1.2 m door clause (row 12).** The fetched text cut off before listing the door options. The outward/sliding/removable options are confirmed only through ArchiPro (row 13) and Blueleaf.
- **The 25 L/s figure** is confirmed through the VBA quoting the NCC, not from the ABCB Part 10.8 page itself, which did not show clause 10.8.2(1) in the fetch.
- **QLD threshold.** The QBCC page mentions $3,300 only for incidental work outside a licence class. A consumer-facing statement that waterproofing over $3,300 needs a QBCC licence was not confirmed. Victoria has no confirmed rule on who may waterproof; the BPC page covers technical rules only.
- **NSW plumbing.** No NSW Fair Trading page quote saying "all plumbing must be done by a licensed plumber" was confirmed. Row 52 (Lismore Council) implies it. For Victoria it is confirmed (rows 50–51).
- **Licensed electricians.** Ausgrid says "qualified electrician", not "licensed". AS/NZS 3000 itself is paywalled, so the zone figures come from the NSW Government summary and are partly from figure captions.
- **WELS star ceilings conflict.** waterrating.gov.au lists "5 stars for showers", while CHOICE says showerheads are rated 1–4 stars. The NSW BASIX page lists flow bands for 4-star showerheads. Avoid stating a maximum star rating for showers.
- **Row 45 (toilet saving).** The 3.5 L / 50 kL / $148 figures come from the extraction model's paraphrase of waterrating.gov.au/node/8. The figures on that page assume a 2014–15 price of $2.99/kL, so they are dated.
- **Home Beautiful "easy ways to add an ensuite" (rows 11, 94, 95).** The names on the page (Nikole Ramsay, John Downs, Michael Tobar, Armelle Habib) look like photographer credits. Attribute the quotes to Home Beautiful, not to those people.
- **Dated costs.** BHG cost figures (row 65) come from 2016–18 surveys. The Sydney figures (rows 58–61) are 2026 but Sydney-only.
- **Not reached:** Oneflare/Airtasker ensuite cost (redirect), hipages direct, ServiceSeeking, Canstar (no usable result), the Archify CS Cavity Sliders article (fetch error), the Architecture & Design ensuite floor-plans and Caroma articles (body not loaded), and Reece, Domain and realestate.com.au small ensuite pages (no WebSearch hit). Bunnings has no ventilation or licensing text.
- **No source found** for how much depth an in-wall cistern saves in mm, or for heated towel rails reducing mould.
