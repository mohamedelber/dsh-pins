## Caveats/unconfirmed

- **Blocked or not fetched:** these permission prompts timed out, so the pages were never read: epa.gov/watersense/showerheads (the EPA showerhead fact-sheet PDF was used instead), finehomebuilding.com "Designing Showers for Small Bathrooms" (ranks #2 to #3), kohler.com "9 small bathroom tips" (cited in Google's AI Overview) and badeloft.com common bathroom sizes.
- **Angi:** no angi.com page was fetched directly. Its numbers appear only through Bob Vila, which cites Angi and HomeAdvisor, and through HomeAdvisor itself (same company). The NerdWallet figure (#133) could not be checked against a primary source.
- **Cost vs Value 2026:** not found. Only the 2025 report was confirmed. Remodeling's 2026 report either isn't published or wasn't indexed. The midrange bath project's spec (room size) was not on the national page.
- **NKBA primary source:** nkba.org/planning-guidelines only sells the book and gives no measurements. All NKBA figures (30 in, 18 in, 36x36, 20 in, 32–43 in, 32 in door) come from Bob Vila and Hunker summaries.
- **IRC code text:** no codes.iccsafe.org page was fetched. Shower 900 sq in / 30 in comes from buildingcodetrainer quoting **IRC P2708.1**, not R307. That is the correct section for shower size; R307.1 is the clearance figure. The 15 in / 21 in toilet figures come from an IPC study guide (open-exam-prep) and a code explainer (learnarchitecture), not from IRC text. The R307.1 and R307.2 wording comes from city handouts (Casa Grande AZ, Richland WA). The P2708.4 text comes from South Carolina's adopted regs on Cornell LII.
- **Not reliable:** fix.com says code requires a 36x36 in shower. That is wrong (IRC is 30 in / 900 sq in), so it was excluded.
- **Contradiction:** This Old House recommends "cool white" 3,000–4,000K bulbs. Golden Lighting and Lighting Reimagined recommend 2700–3000K. The two ALA height figures also differ: 65–70 in (Houzz) versus 60–66 in (Golden Lighting).
- **ENERGY STAR:** energystar.gov was not fetched. The 48% figure is Broan-NuTone citing ENERGY STAR.
- **Wall-hung toilet floor space:** the summariser gave the Duravit "up to 2 square feet" saving garbled, so it was left out of the table. No confirmed inch figure exists for wall-hung toilet savings.
- **Syndicated quotes:** BHG, The Spruce, Good Housekeeping and Southern Living quotes were fetched from AOL.com reposts. Find the original article URLs before citing them.
- **Publications not found:** no usable designer quotes from House Beautiful, Architectural Digest, Veranda, Elle Decor or Real Simple turned up in search. HGTV, Apartment Therapy, This Old House, Southern Living, BHG, The Spruce and Good Housekeeping were used instead.
- **Low-authority sources (lightingreimagined.com, Planner 5D, Dimensions.com):** use them for context only.
- **Old source:** the JLC "Fighting for Inches" article is from 1996. It's fine for the "classic 5x7" history point, but not for current code.
- **IAEI:** its "2014: GFCI required … within 6 ft of any bathtub or shower stall" item refers to areas outside the bathroom (bathrooms were already covered in 1975). It was not used in the table.
- **Not confirmed:** a sourced 3x6 or 4x5 half-bath dimension. Sources give square footage instead: 15–20 sq ft (about 3x5) and 16–27 sq ft.
