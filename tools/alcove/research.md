Notes for the writer:
- Rows 1–3 are the only alcove-size numbers found. No magazine or official source gave a "typical alcove width"; row 1 (depth 300–450 mm) is the best figure. Widths vary, roughly 90–120 cm in the examples.
- No authoritative source confirmed a typical alcove base-cupboard depth of 30–40 cm. Rows 1, 6 and 7 (300–400 mm for storage/media, 300–350 mm drinks) are the closest.
- Desk height: UK sources say 69–71 cm, not 72–75 cm.
- Wickes hidden-bracket ratings are per PAIR (40 kg / 50 kg). The twin-slot rating is per BRACKET (24 kg).
