## Unconfirmed / caveats
- **Temperature 60-67°F**: NOT confirmed. Sleep Foundation (fetched) says ~65°F and 65-68°F. Use those figures.
- **Skin tones (terra cotta, copper, cream)**: HGTV mentions warm earthy skin tones, but no named practitioner and I did not capture an exact quote. Use rows 29-31 instead, or say "HGTV's feng shui guide suggests..." without a quote.
- **Full mattress width**: Sleep Foundation 54 in; Casper lists 53 in. Use 54 in (Sleep Foundation) or "53-54 in".
- **36 in at the foot of the bed / queen needs 10x10 ft room**: Casper page says this, but I captured it only as a paraphrase, not an exact quote. Hunker's 3 ft door-to-furniture figure (row 35) is quoted.
- **No NKBA/AIA bedroom clearance standard found**; clearance figures come from Sleep Foundation (24 in) and Hunker.
- **Real Simple** (SERP #6) blocked fetches; **The Spruce, Architectural Digest, House Beautiful, BHG, Good Housekeeping, Apartment Therapy** pages did not appear in searches as fetchable URLs, so none are used. Homes & Gardens, Livingetc, HGTV, Tom's Guide, Havenly and Anjie Cho stand in.
- Sleep Foundation's /bedroom-environment/blue-light URL was blocked; the /light-and-sleep/blue-light version was used (it also advises electronics off 2-3 hours before bed).
- Tom's Guide wellness article (rows 16, 17; also Roynon's mirrored-wardrobe and coffin/corpse-position comments) is a paid partnership with Nectar Sleep - worth a less prominent mention.
- Wikipedia does not name Lin Yun; the Lin Yun/BTB origin comes from Feng Shui by Jen (a practitioner blog).
- Homes & Gardens places the bagua "Love" area in the southwest (compass method); "far right corner" (BTB) not confirmed.
