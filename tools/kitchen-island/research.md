## Caveats

- **Price drift.** TORNVIKEN costs £379 on IKEA GB today, but Homebuilding (Feb 2023) quotes £329. VADHOLMA is £429/£579 now, but Ideal Home says "less than £350" (older). The Dunelm Francis is £349 on Dunelm today but £249 in Ideal Home's 23 Sep 2026 piece, probably a sale. Use the live retailer prices and add a "prices checked Oct 2026" line.
- **IKEA GB naming.** IKEA labels sizes as "Length/Width" instead of width/depth. For FÖRHÖJA, 100 cm is the length and 43 cm is the depth. TORNVIKEN's 77 cm figure is its depth.
- **STENSTORP and BEKVÄM.** No current IKEA GB listing turned up for STENSTORP; it looks discontinued in the UK, so don't feature it. No GB page or price turned up for the BEKVÄM trolley; only non-UK IKEA pages appeared. Treat it as unverified for the UK.
- **John Lewis.** The own-brand butcher's trolley is discontinued. The Eddingtons Lambourn page (p435538) was blocked (PROVENANCE_REQUIRED, not fetched), so its £875 price comes only from Ideal Home's list.
- **Unit conversions in Livingetc.** The "can I fit an island" article gives designers' figures in inches (47 in, 35 in, 31 in, 23 in). These are likely conversions from the original mm/cm (≈120 cm, 90 cm, 80 cm, 60 cm), so don't quote the inches as the designers' own words.
- **Clearance figures vary by source.** They run from 80 cm (Masterclass, Proudfoot, no appliances) through 90 cm (Wickes, DIY Kitchens) and 1 m (Wren, Harvey Jones, most designers) to 1.2 m (Searle & Taylor, Life Kitchens, two cooks). Present 1 m as the working minimum and 1.2 m for two cooks or a main route. The Size Calculator page should own the detail.
- **Breakfast-bar height conflict.** Homebuilding says a raised section at 1060 mm, DFS says 100–110 cm, and Homes & Gardens says 1200 mm "if raised above the worktop". The 105 cm in the brief sits within the DFS range. Treat 1200 mm as an outlier.
- **Socket height claim.** Ideal Home quotes Karl Bantleman (Direct365) saying sockets "by law should be placed 15cm above" the worktop. No regulation found supports "by law". NICEIC says "ideally" 10 cm, and the 30 cm from a sink is guidance, not a statutory distance. Don't repeat the "by law" wording.
- **Electrical Safety First.** No ESF page on island sockets or kitchens turned up in search results, so none was fetched. NICEIC (the UK certification body) and the government's Approved Document P stand in. The AD P PDF fetched is the 2013 edition and cites BS 7671:2008+A1. BS 7671 is now at the 18th Edition (2018 + amendments), so cite "BS 7671" without an edition year. Part P applies in England (and Wales in similar form). Scotland uses building warrants.
- **BKBA/KBSA.** Nothing usable turned up. No BKBA or KBSA figures are included.
- **Magnet/Howdens.** Their island pages are product and inspiration pages with no measurements. Magnet's figures here come via its Head of Design, quoted in Ideal Home. The Howdens pan-drawer price needs a trade login.
- **Pan drawer internal sizes.** No source gave internal pan-drawer heights. Howdens gives the front height (275 mm nominal, 342 mm actual).
- **Cost sources.** MyJobQuote (Oct 2026) is current. Homebuilding (2023/2025), Ideal Home (updated April 2025) and Livingetc (June 2025) are 1–3 years old. Trade2Base is a trade-pricing blog. Davonport and Livingetc's £3,000+ and £10–20k figures are high-end, so don't present them as typical.
- **Livingetc spelling.** In the cost article Livingetc spells the brand "Devonport" but the designer "Davonport". The brand is Davonport.
- **Masterclass stat.** "in 2026, 42% of UK homeowners opted for island that were seven feet or longer" has no cited source and looks odd. It is excluded.
- **AOL.** No AOL syndicated copies were used.

## SERP notes

- **The SERP mixes two kinds of page.** Brand and retailer pages (Wren, Howdens, Magnet, IKEA GB, DIY.com) sell or inspire. Magazine galleries (House & Garden, Pinterest, plus Ideal Home and Livingetc for the small-kitchen variants) show pictures. Sweeten is a US remodelling site.
- **Wren** (/kitchens/kitchen-islands) is a conversion page. It names island types (peninsula, small, double) and features (open shelving, pop-up sockets, island wine rack) but gives **no measurements or prices**.
- **Howdens** (/kitchen-island-ideas) gives 10 style ideas (handleless, gloss, matt, curves and so on) with **no measurements and no real storage content**.
- **Magnet** (/all-kitchen-islands) is a product grid of 12 of its 19 ranges, with finance offers and **no planning guidance**.
- **Gap to exploit.** None of the brand pages pair ideas with real sizes, £ prices and storage specifics. The magazine pieces (Ideal Home's 12 small island ideas, Livingetc's 11 ways) have designer quotes but few products or prices. A list that gives each idea a product, its size in cm, its price and "the storage it adds" is clearly different.
- **Format.** About 10–13 ideas is the norm (Howdens 10, Livingetc 11, Ideal Home 12). Twelve fits.
- **Small-kitchen variants.** The "small kitchen island ideas" and "…for small kitchens" queries (KD18–23) have lower difficulty. Ideal Home and Livingetc rank with designer-quote listicles. Cover peninsula, trolley, slimline and drop-leaf explicitly to match that intent.
- **Supporting questions to answer briefly** (FAQ candidates): how much space around an island (1 m / 1.2 m), the minimum island size (1000 x 1000 mm), whether you can put a hob on a small island, socket rules, pendant height, and cost. Link the sizing questions to the Kitchen Island Size Calculator.
