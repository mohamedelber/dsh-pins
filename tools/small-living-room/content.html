The Small Living Room Spacing Guide

A small room feels comfortable when the spaces between things are right. Wayfair's guide allows between 12" – 18" of space between the table and the sofa.

How to Plan a Small Apartment Living Room in 6 Steps

  1. Measure the room. Note the windows, door swing, radiators and outlets.
  2. Decide the main job. TV, conversation, guests or work: let one thing lead the layout.
  3. Place the sofa first. Usually against the longest wall or facing the TV wall.
  4. Mark the walkway. Keep a 30 to 36 in path from the door through the room.
  5. Add the rug and coffee table. Front legs on the rug, table 16 to 18 in from the sofa.
  6. Go vertical. Put storage, the TV and lights on the walls to keep the floor clear.

Furniture Ideas for a Small Apartment Living Room

1. An Apartment-Size Sofa on Legs

Look for a sofa about 70 to 84 in wide with slim arms and raised legs. Seeing the floor underneath makes the room feel larger, and slim arms give you more seat in the same width.

2. A Round Coffee Table

A round table takes up less visual space, has no corners to bump into and makes it easier to walk around. Our coffee table decor ideas show how to style it without clutter.

3. Nesting Tables

A pair of nesting tables works as one coffee table most days and pulls apart into two when friends come over. One can slide out to become a side table or a laptop stand.

4. Armless Chairs and Poufs

Armless chairs, a small swivel chair or a pair of poufs add seats without bulk. Poufs tuck under a console when you need the floor back.

5. Sofa Against the Wall or Floating

In a very small room, push the sofa against the wall to free the middle. In a slightly bigger room, floating it a little off the wall with a slim console behind can define the living area, especially in an open-concept apartment.

6. Furniture That Stores

A storage ottoman, a coffee table with a shelf or drawers, and a sofa with a storage base all hide blankets, games and chargers without adding another piece of furniture.

Small Living Room Storage Ideas

7. A Wall of Shelves Around the TV

Shelves that run around and above the TV turn one wall into storage, with closed cabinets at the bottom for clutter. Anchor them: the CPSC says to anchor TVs and furniture, such as bookcases and dressers, securely to the wall. For styling, see our bookshelf decor ideas.

8. A Floating TV Console

A wall-mounted console keeps the floor visible and makes cleaning easier. Mount it into studs with hardware rated for the load, and keep it below the TV so cables stay hidden.

9. Baskets and Lidded Boxes

Woven baskets under a console or on a low shelf hold throws, remotes and kids' toys. Lidded boxes keep the look calm, which matters more in a small room.

10. Renter-Friendly Wall Hanging

If you cannot drill, adhesive strips work for light frames and small shelves only. 3M says to use four pairs of Large Command strips to securely hang frames weighing up to 15 pounds; always check the weight limit on the pack, and never use strips for anything heavy or above a seat.

Ways to Make a Small Living Room Feel Bigger

11. A Big Mirror

Home Depot's small living room guide notes that mirrors amplify the available light while creating the impression of more space. Place one opposite or beside a window, and secure a floor mirror to the wall so it is much less likely to tip.

12. Curtains Hung High and Wide

Hang the rod close to the ceiling and extend it past the window frame so the curtains frame the glass instead of covering it. Our curtain size calculator tells you the width and length to buy.

13. Light, Calm Walls

Warm white, cream or a soft sage on the walls bounces light around. Keep the ceiling white and repeat one or two accent colors in cushions and art.

14. A Rug That Is Big Enough

Ruggable's guide says to ensure that the front legs of your seating fit on the rug, and for furniture placed against the walls, Home Depot suggests a 6' x 9' or 8' x 10' rug size for living room spaces. A rug that is too small makes the room look smaller. Our rug size calculator works it out for your layout.

15. Layered, Wall-Mounted Lighting

Swap a big floor lamp for a plug-in wall sconce, add a table lamp and keep the ceiling light for cleaning. Light at different heights makes corners feel part of the room.

Living Room Ideas That Do Double Duty

16. A Small Dining Nook

A round pedestal table for two in a corner or by the window gives you a place to eat without a separate dining room. Our dining table size calculator shows what fits and how much space chairs need.

17. A Slim Work Corner

A narrow wall desk or a console behind the sofa can be a home office. Choose a chair that tucks fully under, and keep work things in one box you can put away at night.

18. A Light Divider

Small Living Room Safety Tips

  • Anchor tall pieces: the CPSC says to anchor TVs and furniture, such as bookcases and dressers, securely to the wall, especially in homes with children; the CPSC credits anchoring with fewer tip-overs.
  • Cords: run cords along walls or behind furniture, not across walkways or under rugs.
  • Rugs: use a non-slip rug pad so a rug is less likely to slide on a hard floor.
  • Mirrors: fix floor and wall mirrors securely, and never rely on adhesive strips for heavy ones.
  • Keep exits clear: do not block the door or a window you might need to open.

Common Small Living Room Mistakes

  • A sofa that is too big. Measure the room and the doorway before you buy.
  • A tiny rug. A rug that only fits under the coffee table makes the room look smaller.
  • Pushing every piece against the walls. In some rooms, floating the seating a little feels cozier.
  • Too many small decor items. Fewer, larger pieces look calmer.
  • Blocking the walkway. Keep the main path at least 30 in wide where you can.

Small Apartment Living Room FAQ

How do I arrange a small apartment living room?

Start with the sofa against the longest wall or facing the TV, keep a 30 to 36 in walkway from the door, put a rug under the front legs of every seat and place the coffee table 16 to 18 in from the sofa. Use the walls for the TV, storage and lights.

What size sofa is best for a small living room?

An apartment-size sofa about 70 to 84 in wide with slim arms and raised legs suits most small living rooms. Measure the room, the doorway and any stairs before you buy, and leave room for a walkway.

How can I make my small living room look bigger?

Use a large mirror near a window, hang curtains high and wide, keep the walls light, choose furniture on legs and a rug big enough for the front legs of every seat. Fewer, larger pieces also make the room feel calmer and more open.

What size rug for a small living room?

Choose a rug big enough for the front legs of the sofa and chairs. In many small living rooms that means a 6 x 9 ft rug, or 8 x 10 ft if the room allows; a rug that only fits under the coffee table usually looks too small.

How do I add storage to a small living room?

Go vertical with shelves around the TV and closed cabinets below, use a storage ottoman or a coffee table with drawers, and add baskets under a console. Anchor tall shelves and the TV to the wall.

Final Thoughts

The best small apartment living room ideas follow a few simple rules: the right spacing, a sofa that fits, a rug big enough for every seat and storage on the walls. Add a mirror, high curtains and warm light, and even a small living room feels open, calm and easy to live in.
