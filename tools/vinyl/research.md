### Notes for the writer
- **Shelf-load math, my calculation, not a quote:** LoC gives 35-45 lb per shelf-foot. A KALLAX cube is about 13 in wide, roughly 1.08 ft. At LoC density, a full cube would hold about 38-49 lb, more than IKEA's "Max load/shelf 29 lb". The keepthemspinning figure (~80 LPs = 35-40 lb) also exceeds 29 lb. Many collectors fill KALLAX cubes anyway. To stay on the safe side, say "check the rated load, and don't cram cubes full of heavy 180 g pressings". Don't claim KALLAX is rated for a full cube of LPs.
- **The temperature figures differ by institution.** LoC gives 65-70°F / 45-50% RH for medium-term storage, and 45-50°F for long-term. NARA gives 55-70°F / 30-55% RH. Gaylord gives 65-70°F / under 50% RH. PSAP gives 40-54°F for archival storage. For a home article, use the LoC medium-term figure and the NARA range, and stress that steady conditions matter more than exact numbers.
- **Basements:** NARA does not ban them. It says they are darker, cooler and more stable, but to avoid them if they flood. Attics and garages are the places it says to avoid.
- **Blocked or not fetched (not used):** loc.gov/preservation/care/record.html (the main LoC care page; its key content is covered by the LoC "Nutshell" PDF above), preservationequipment.com, kbaudio.co.uk, blog.crackerbarrel.com, primestorage.com, stylebyemilyhenderson.com, and the KALLAX 4x4 US page. The NEDCC AV textbook (ch. 1 sec. 8) has no disc storage guidance. The NARA Prologue 2007 article has none either.
