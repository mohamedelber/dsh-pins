Run a home, interiors, moving or DIY blog? You can add any of our free home calculators to your own website. Each one works inside your page, in inches or centimeters, and gives your readers an instant answer without leaving your site.

The calculators are free to use on blogs and websites. All we ask is that you keep the small credit link under the calculator, which is already part of the code.

How to Add a Calculator to Your Site

  1. Open Embed this calculator under the tool you want and press Copy code.
  2. In WordPress, add a Custom HTML block where you want the calculator. On other platforms, use the HTML or embed option.
  3. Paste the code and publish. The calculator adapts to the width of your page.
