    How High Should a TV Be Mounted?

    The simple rule is to put the center of the screen at the eye level of the person watching. Home technology trade site CE Pro recommends mounting a TV so the center is at eye level when you are seated, and gives about 42 inches (107 cm) from the floor to the center of the screen as a rule of thumb. That is the starting point the calculator uses for the Sofa option.

    If the TV cannot sit at eye level, keep the angle small. CE Pro advises no more than 15 degrees of vertical angle to the viewer, and MantelMount's guide to eye-level TV viewing cites THX guidance to keep the vertical angle within 15 degrees of the center of the screen, treating it as a limit, not a target. The calculator checks this angle for you and turns red when it is exceeded.

    TV Height Chart for a Sofa

    TV Height for a Bedroom

    In bed you usually sit higher and lean back, so a bedroom TV often goes a little higher than in a living room. Eye heights in bed vary a lot with the bed and the pillows, so sit where you watch, measure from the floor to your eyes and enter it under More options.     Mounting a TV Above a Fireplace

    A mantel usually forces the screen well above eye level, which is why CE Pro suggests drop-down or tilting mounts and MantelMount recommends a pull-down mount for this spot. Check the fireplace box in the calculator, enter the lowest the bottom of the TV can go (the top of the mantel plus the clearance in your fireplace and TV manuals) and it shows the real viewing angle. If it is over 15 degrees, a pull-down mount, sitting farther back or another wall will be more comfortable. Styling the mantel underneath? See our Christmas mantel ideas.

    Mount It Safely

    A wall mount only works if it holds. Use a mount rated for your TV's weight and screen size, fix it into studs or with anchors meant for your wall type, and follow the TV and mount instructions. The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's Anchor It campaign advises mounting flat-screen TVs to the wall or to furniture to stop them toppling over, and anchoring TVs that are not wall-mounted to the wall. The campaign aims to prevent tip-overs from killing and injuring children.

    TV Mounting Height FAQ

    How high should a TV be mounted from the floor?

    Mount the TV so the center of the screen is at the seated eye level of the viewer, which is often around 42 inches (107 cm) from the floor for a standard sofa. A 55 inch TV centered there has its bottom edge about 29 inches up and its top edge about 55 inches up.

    Where should the center of a TV be?

    The center of the screen should line up with your eyes when you sit where you usually watch. If that is not possible, keep the angle from your eyes up to the center of the screen under 15 degrees, which CE Pro, and THX guidance cited by MantelMount, give as the maximum.

    Is a TV above a fireplace too high?

    Often, yes. A mantel usually pushes the center of the screen well above seated eye level, and the viewing angle can go past 15 degrees. Use the fireplace option in the calculator to check the real angle, and consider a pull-down mount, sitting farther back or another wall.

    How high should a TV be in a bedroom?

    A bedroom TV usually goes a little higher than a living room TV because you sit higher in bed. Sit up the way you normally watch, measure from the floor to your eyes and put the center of the screen at that height, or slightly above if you lean back on pillows.
