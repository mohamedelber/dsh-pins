## Summary

- **We showed up in the live SERP for only 5 of 15 keyword/market checks.** Even then, we were never on mobile page 1 above the fold. Best result: airing cupboard storage ideas, UK mobile, organic #5 (absolute position 10, under 9 blocks). The other 4: under stairs door (organic #10), gift bag storage (organic #12 to #13), beanie storage (desktop only, organic #20) and european laundry (desktop only, organic #13).
- **AI overviews never cite us. We are in no image pack, video or short-video block.** Every AIO reference list is brands, Amazon, Reddit, TikTok or a few blogs.
- **Most of these head SERPs are shop, brand or UGC.** Gift bag storage, beanie storage, small door for under stairs, under stairs storage, wallpaper calculator (both markets) and laundry room closets are RED: there is no real "blog" lane on page 1.
- **The SERPs that do have a blog lane** are YELLOW: laundry closet, ideas to store handbags, kitchen island size calculator and airing cupboard. On those, rank is the lever, not the SERP.
- **GSC and the live SERPs disagree.** Several pages (hat, purse, kitchen island, wallpaper) have a good GSC average position (4 to 8) on head queries that we could not find in the live top 25 to 30. Their impressions mostly come from AI Mode / conversational queries (lots of "show me pictures"-type rows at position 1 to 3, excluded here), from anonymised long-tail queries, or from day-to-day volatility. The airing SERP changed between two pulls minutes apart: YouTube #1 versus Amazon #1, and we were absent in one and at #5 in the other.
- **Pattern: our clicks come from long-tail, specific queries, not the head terms.** Examples: "how to store hats in small spaces", "european laundry ideas" (11% CTR), "hot water cupboard shelving". The best lever is usually to point titles and H1s at the specific long-tail query the page already wins, not to chase the head term.
- **Index check.** color-coded-closet, kitchen-island-size-calculator and wallpaper-calculator are all indexed. Two things to look at:
  - wallpaper-calculator shows no sitemap. Its only referring URL is /editorial-policy/.
  - kitchen-island-size-calculator is referred only by /baseboard-calculator/.

### Method notes
- The "what is above us" order is the SERP item order. On mobile, popular_products blocks sometimes carry their own rank numbering, so I placed them by list position.
- On mobile continuous scroll, DataForSEO sometimes repeats page-1 results on page 2 (seen in diy closet shelves and ideas to store handbags). Composition counts include those repeats as listed.
- "Not in top N" is a single live snapshot. GSC average positions blend many days and query variants, and include image-pack and AI-mode appearances, so the two can legitimately differ.
- Raw SERP JSON for each check is saved in this session's scratchpad (`*_mobile.json`, `*_desk.json`).
