### Addition D: 4 new FAQ entries (PAA) — gap 3
Add them at the top of the existing "Laundry Closet FAQ" in this order; keep all 7 current FAQs.
1. **What is a laundry closet (and what else is it called)?** It's a washer and dryer, stacked or side by side, inside a closet or alcove behind doors. It's also called a laundry nook or laundry alcove. Source: Block Renovation lists "Laundry closet (stacked)" at 3.5–4 ft x 3 ft and "(side-by-side)" at 5 x 3 ft (URL above). (The definition is ours; the sizes are cited.)
2. **How deep does a laundry closet need to be?** Full-size front-loaders need 38–40 in (32–34 in machine + 6 in), compact units 30–32 in, plus about 4 ft clear in front. A standard 24-in reach-in is too shallow for full-size units. Sources: Whirlpool sizing guide; ClearClosets "minimum of 30 to 34 inches of interior depth" (URLs above).
3. **How much does it cost to build a laundry closet?** Under $325 for a DIY refresh; $500–5,000 to relocate hookups; $4,000–8,000 for a custom built-in. Sources: Four Generations, HomeAdvisor, ClearClosets (URLs above).
4. **What should you not store in a laundry closet?** Don't keep boxes, piles of clothing or other things that burn next to the dryer. Never dry items that have touched oil, gasoline, paint thinner or alcohol. Keep pods and detergent out of children's reach (link to idea #17). Source: NFPA dryer and washer safety tips, https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2016/06/f32/NFPA_DryerWasherSafetyTips.pdf: "Keep the dryer area clear of things that can burn, such as boxes or clothing." Never dry items "that have come in contact with flammable substances, such as cooking oil, gasoline, paint thinner, alcohol." "The leading cause of home clothes dryer and washer fires is failure to clean them."
