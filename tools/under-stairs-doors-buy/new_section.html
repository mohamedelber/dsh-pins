Where to Buy Under Stairs Cupboard Doors (and What They Cost)

Standard internal doors are rectangular, so on their own they only fit the tall, square part of an under stairs opening. For the sloping part you need a door with an angled top: you can order one made to measure online, have a joiner make it, or build it yourself. Before you order, check these points, because they decide the real cost:

  • The measurements they need. Made-to-measure suppliers ask for the height at each end of the door and its width (Doors Sincerely also asks for the top width if it differs from the bottom). These match H1, H2 and W in the diagram above, but take them for each door, not for the whole opening.
  • The finish. Most oak doors arrive unfinished, so budget for oil, varnish or paint. Wittswood, for example, charges £60 extra for an oiled finish.
  • Hinges and handles. These are often extra; Wittswood sells its hand-forged ironmongery pack for £42.23.
  • Returns. According to Citizens Advice, the usual 14-day cooling-off period for online orders does not apply to items that are bespoke or made to measure, so measure twice before you pay.
  • Fitting. Hanging an internal door costs about £80 to £160 in labour, and a carpenter typically charges £180 to £320 a day across most of the UK, or £280 to £450 in London and the South East. Angled doors, new frames and panelling take longer than a standard door.
