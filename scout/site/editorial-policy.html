Editorial Policy

Daily Space Hacks publishes practical guides to organizing and storing things in real homes, especially small ones. This page explains how our guides are made, how we use AI tools, and how we check what we publish. It was last updated on October 3, 2026.

Who Is Behind the Site

Daily Space Hacks is run by Mohamed Elber. He chooses the topics, decides what each guide should cover and is responsible for everything published on the site. You can read more on our About page or reach us through the Contact page.

How We Use AI Tools

We are open about this: our guides are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools. AI helps us organize research, draft text, build our measurement diagrams and create illustrative images. It does not decide what we publish. Every guide is planned around a real question people ask, and every factual claim is checked before the guide goes live.

How We Fact-Check

  • Safety and health claims (for example dryer fire risks, furniture tip-overs, mold or holiday lights) are checked against official or expert sources such as the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, the National Fire Protection Association, the EPA, university extension services and museum conservation institutes. We link to the source inside the guide so you can check it yourself.
  • Measurements are given as typical ranges in inches and centimeters. Products and homes vary, so we remind readers to check their own space and the manufacturer’s instructions.
  • Titles, descriptions and image alt text are written and reviewed by hand so they describe the page accurately.
  • We avoid overclaims. If an idea does not suit renters, small budgets or every type of home, we say so.

About Our Images

The room photos in our guides are AI-generated illustrations, created to show an idea clearly and in a consistent style. They are not photos of real customer homes and do not show specific branded products. Our diagrams are original and made for this site.

Corrections and Updates

We update guides when better information becomes available. If you spot a mistake, an outdated fact or a broken link, please tell us through the Contact page and we will review it and correct the guide if needed.

Independence and Affiliate Links

Our advice is written to be useful first. The site may show Google AdSense ads, which help keep it free. We do not currently use affiliate links or publish sponsored posts, and no brand pays to be mentioned. Ads never decide what we recommend. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.
