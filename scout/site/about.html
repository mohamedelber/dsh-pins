About Us

Small Spaces, Smarter Storage

Daily Space Hacks is a home organization blog for people who live in small spaces: apartments, rentals, studios, and compact homes where every inch counts.

Most organizing advice assumes you have a walk-in closet, a big pantry, and permission to drill into every wall. Most of us don’t. We focus on practical storage ideas that work in real small homes, often on a budget and often without tools or permanent changes.

What You’ll Find Here

  • Small bathroom storage: under-sink, over-the-toilet, door and shower ideas for tiny bathrooms.
  • Closet and wardrobe organization: ways to fit more into small closets, or live well with no closet at all.
  • Small kitchen and pantry hacks: cabinet, counter and no-pantry solutions.
  • Renter-friendly storage: no-drill, damage-free ideas that protect your deposit.
  • DIY storage projects: simple, affordable builds and upcycles.

How We Create Our Content

Every guide is built to be genuinely useful, not just long. Before we publish, we:

  • Focus on real problems people search for, like a bathroom with no cabinets or a kitchen with no pantry.
  • Include practical details: measurements, what fits where, and what to avoid.
  • Check safety points, such as furniture anchoring, fire safety and loft loading, against official sources and link to them.
  • Update articles when we find better solutions or when information changes.

We do not currently use affiliate links or sponsored posts. The site may show ads to help keep it free, and they never change what we recommend. You can read more in our Affiliate Disclosure.

Meet the Writer

Mohamed Elber is the founder of Daily Space Hacks. Based in Morocco, he has always loved organizing spaces, especially small ones where every inch has to work hard. He started this blog to share practical, budget-friendly storage ideas that help people make the most of small homes, apartments and rentals.

He researches and reviews every guide before it goes live, checks facts against trusted sources, and updates articles when better solutions come along.
